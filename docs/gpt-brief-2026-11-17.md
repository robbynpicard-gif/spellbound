# TypeL: where we are, and the scoring review (brief for design discussion)

_2026-11-17 · game version 0.17.1 · live at https://robbynpicard-gif.github.io/TypeL/_

This brief is for fleshing out the design outside the codebase. Bring the
decisions back as answers to section 6. Everything below describes the game as
it actually runs today.

---

## 1. The game in one paragraph

TypeL is a browser typing game that reimagines *Typing Maniac*. A wizard
sits at a brass typewriter in a gothic study. Words fall past the arched
windows on parchment scrolls; you type a word and press **Enter** to capture it
before it reaches the window sill (the **ward line**). Each word that breaks
through damages the ward; if the ward is fully destroyed (100% instability), the
run ends. Coloured **spell scrolls** store mana; typing a spell's name and Enter
casts it.

**Tone:** a serious, dark world that doesn't take itself too seriously.
Celebrations are big and a bit absurd (Peggle-like). Lore is implied, never
told. Darkness is "the world forgetting itself" and is shown through absence,
not monsters.

## 2. Where the project stands

**In the game now**
- **Campaign:** one run of 20 levels in 4 chapters (about 20 minutes). Levels 5, 10, 15 and 20 are bosses where you survive 60–120 s while the pace ramps up.
  - Normal mode restores the ward each level.
  - Hardcore mode carries ward damage through the run; each boss heals 25%.
  - Score and combo carry from level to level.
  - After each boss you pick 1 of 3 spells as a reward.
- **Endless Practice:** any unlocked level, no end, with personal bests.
- **Wizard Duels:** multiplayer; the server is written but not hosted.
- **8 spells**, unlocked one by one across the campaign: Slow, Ice, Barrier, Shock, Alter, Fire, Wind, Harvest. They share one mana pool of 8.
- **Scoring:** combo multiplier tiers, bonuses, and a 2-second level-end celebration with the score counting up (details in section 3).
- **Stats screen:** lifetime totals and records.
- **Sound** (all synthesised):
  - The music changes with play: it darkens as the ward weakens and brightens with bells and choir as the combo climbs. Changes land on the beat.
  - The typewriter sounds brassy and clunky.
  - Every spell cast is a big moment: a split-second freeze, the spell's name slams in, a shockwave, a screen kick, a bass drop, and a unique animation per spell.

**Shelved (built, switched off)**
- A roster of 6 playable wizards with unique powers, plus per-wizard cosmetic unlock tracks. The owner felt this was drifting from the core.

**The direction now**
1. Make the **core moment-to-moment loop** more engaging.
2. **One wizard that belongs to the player** and grows with them. To be designed.
3. **Art style:** four sample directions exist (Ink & Wash Gothic, Illuminated Manuscript, Stained Glass, Cut-Paper Shadow Theatre). Not chosen yet. The current game is dark realistic-lit pixel art.

**Other wishes on record**
- Addictive loops without gambling or dark patterns, and never a barrier to playing.
- A universal leaderboard (needs hosting).
- Possibly Steam later.
- A Daily Challenge with unlimited attempts.

## 3. Scoring today, exactly

### 3.1 Points for a word
`points = 10 × letters × (1 + bonus %) × multiplier`, plus any flat bonus × multiplier.

**Worked example:** "lantern" (7 letters), caught with no typo (Perfect +25%) high on the screen (Quick +20%) at ×3:
70 × 1.45 × 3 = **305**.

### 3.2 The combo multiplier
Every captured word adds 1 to the combo. The multiplier rises in tiers:

| Combo | 0–4 | 5–11 | 12–24 | 25–44 | 45+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multiplier | ×1 | ×2 | ×3 | ×5 | ×10 |

Each new tier gets a fanfare, a proclamation on screen and brighter music. In a Campaign run the combo carries into the next level, but only if you won the level.

### 3.3 Word bonuses (added to the base before the multiplier)

| Bonus | When | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect | no typo while typing that word | +25% |
| Quick | caught in the top 30% of the fall | +20% |
| Clutch | caught in the last 15% before the ward | +50% |
| Arcane Word | rare letters (j k q x z, v w y), 3+ consonants in a row | +15% per point, max +60% |
| Long Word | 9+ letters | +20% |
| Name-Caller | a capitalised legend name (Merlin, Odin…) | +30% |
| Volley | 3+ captures within 2 s | +10% per capture past 2, max +50% |
| Alliteration | same first letter as the previous capture | +15% per link, max +60% |
| Librarian | captures in alphabetical order (3+) | +10% per link past 2, max +50% |
| Metronome | your last 16 keystrokes were evenly spaced | +15% |
| Clean Sweep | the screen is empty after 3+ captures | flat 100 × multiplier |

### 3.4 Level bonuses (on a won level, as a share of that level's points)
- **Flawless:** no misses, +25%.
- **Under Par:** finished faster than a par time, +15%.
- **Comeback:** the ward was at 75%+ instability at some point and you still won, +20%.

### 3.5 Points from spells
Spells score less, on purpose:
- **Shock:** 4 words at base points with no multiplier.
- **Harvest:** every word on screen at half base.
- **Fire:** burns everything for no points; it's a bailout.

None of the spells change the combo.

### 3.6 What mistakes cost today

| Mistake | Combo | Other cost |
|---|---|---|
| A **typo**: a key that makes the text match nothing (fixed with Backspace) | kept | that word loses Perfect and **falls faster** (+12 to +24 px/s, rising by level) |
| **Enter on a wrong or incomplete word** | drops **one tier** (e.g. 30 → 12) | the word the game guesses you meant falls faster, and an error is counted |
| A **word breaks through the ward** | **reset to 0** | the ward takes 10% (level 1) to 15% (level 20) damage; about 7–10 misses ends the run |
| Pressing Enter with a **spell name and no mana** | none | a "fizzle" sound |

Accuracy is correct keystrokes ÷ all letter keystrokes. WPM uses the standard 5 letters per word. Both are shown live; neither changes your score.

## 4. Observations from the current build

1. **Too many bonuses to understand.** Eleven word bonuses, each a small percentage, add up to a total nobody can read while playing. Perfect, Quick and Clutch are instantly readable; Librarian, Metronome and Arcane mostly arrive as surprises. Quick and Clutch also pull against each other: one rewards speed, the other rewards waiting.
2. **Small gaps between mistake penalties.** A typo you fix is nearly free, apart from the word speeding up, which most players won't notice. A wrong Enter costs a tier, which feels harsh at ×10 (45 → 25) and trivial at ×2.
3. **Little reason to type well rather than fast.** Accuracy isn't rewarded beyond Perfect's +25%. There's no streak of clean words that is separate from the combo.
4. **The multiplier is the real engine.** Bonuses are noise next to ×10, so the core skill being rewarded is "never let a word through". That may be right, but it should be a deliberate choice.
5. **Spells and score are disconnected.** Casting doesn't feed the score loop, apart from avoiding misses.
6. **No "near-miss" drama.** Clutch exists, but nothing on screen makes a last-second catch feel heroic, apart from a label.

## 5. Ideas on the table (not decided)

**Rewarding good typing**
- A **Clean Streak**: consecutive words with zero typos, shown separately, that boosts the multiplier ("on fire" state).
- A **Perfect tier**: e.g. 10 perfect words in a row doubles the current multiplier until the next typo.
- **Rhythm rewards**, a clearer Metronome: steady typing builds a visible "flow" meter.
- **Speed tiers**: bigger Quick bonuses the earlier you catch a word, shown as distance-based text.
- **Overkill**: capturing a word long before it falls adds "time" to a meter that fuels a power.

**Punishing mistakes (fairly)**
- A **typo costs a small chunk of a combo meter** instead of nothing; Backspace still saves the streak.
- A **wrong Enter** costs a fixed amount, e.g. −5 combo, instead of a whole tier.
- A **miss** keeps the reset to 0, but a "last chance" save is earned by good play.
- **Visible penalties:** the word that speeds up flashes and shows "+speed"; the combo visibly cracks.

**Making the loop more engaging**
- **Special words:**
  - armoured (type twice);
  - splitting (breaks into two short words);
  - ghost (fades in and out);
  - linked pairs (capture both within 2 s for a bonus);
  - bomb (must be typed before others or it damages more).
- **Bosses** with their own tricks: a boss that reverses or scrambles words, or one that shakes the screen.
- **Pressure within a level:** a final wave or rush at the end of each level.
- **Score feel:** a bigger "×10" fever state with its own look and sound.

## 6. Decisions needed

1. **Bonuses:** keep all 11, cut to a core few (e.g. Perfect, Quick, Clutch, Volley, Clean Sweep), or replace them with new systems (Clean Streak, Flow)?
2. **What is the main skill rewarded:** never missing (the multiplier), typing cleanly (accuracy), typing fast (speed), or a mix? In what order of importance?
3. **Typos:** should a fixed typo cost anything beyond losing Perfect? If yes, what?
4. **Wrong Enter:** a whole tier down, a fixed combo loss, or something else?
5. **Misses:** keep the reset to 0? Any way to earn a save?
6. **Spells and score:** should casting at the right moment be rewarded (e.g. a big Shock combo), or should spells stay as survival tools?
7. **Your own wizard:**
   - What grows: looks only (fair leaderboards), small permanent upgrades, or one upgrade picked per run?
   - What earns it: total points, or milestones?
8. **Core loop priority:** special word types, boss mechanics, clearer mistake feedback, or more pressure within levels? Which first?
9. **Leaderboard fairness:** if upgrades change the game, do they get a separate leaderboard (or upgrades off in ranked play)?
