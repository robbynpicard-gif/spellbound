## Vision and emotional promise
Build the **definitive, exhilarating, addictive-through-mastery arcane typing game**: premium modern indie feel, tactile rapid typing, readable falling enchanted scrolls, escalating combat pressure, exciting Combo/Arcane Volley and spell synergies, huge *earned* payoffs, and atmospheric wizardly wonder. Emotional arc: discovery → typing rhythm → flow → danger → spectacular skillful release → recovery → mastery. This is a typing game FIRST, not a passive fantasy animation, spreadsheet, carnival or literal casino. No gambling economy, dark patterns, artificial prohibitions, or unreadable spectacle.

## Approved world and visual identity
**Arcane / Alchemical Renaissance**, not generic fantasy or Gothic-grit-by-default. Warm aged parchment, illuminated manuscripts, enchanted ink, wax seals, dark carved wood, engraved brass/copper, glass laboratory vessels, mechanical keys and gears, arcane runes, candles and amber light. Wizard's tower/study, alchemist's workshop, underground laboratories and magical libraries should feel like believable functional spaces. Eccentric, scholarly, expressive wizards and strange machinery. Pipe-smoking wizard was an evocative reference, not a requirement or final character design. Rich atmosphere with high contrast where the player reads and types. Elemental accents should have distinct visual identities; never wash out scroll letters.

**Art style distinction:** On Nov 16 the owner liked a pixel-art reference featuring warm parchment, dark magical-library space and an alchemical typewriter. Later the owner explicitly approved a significant **Renaissance workstation visual/layout/audio overhaul**. Thus pixel art is a historical approved reference, **not a locked final rendering technique**. Older gritty Gothic sprites, fixed coordinates and exact HUD are NOT binding. Do not insist on low-res sprites, generic fantasy UI or muddy darkness. Make a coherent attractive prototype; mark final rendering style and character portraits unresolved, not approved.

## Required gameplay layout / physical composition
One-screen 2D combat. A **large, clearly readable falling-scroll battlefield** is the primary action zone. A **prominent physical magical typewriter / writing desk** anchors the lower scene: the player's wizard is situated at the workstation, and the desk is a real mechanical focal point, not a decorative afterthought. A spell rack/charges, Ward, score, Combo, Volley, quota and All-In status belong in the scene, integrated into desk/arcane apparatus where practical, but must be glanceable. The old lower-left wizard/desk-side-rack arrangement is inspiration, **not immutable placement**. Recompose the scene if it improves readability and feel. Do not just recolor old boxes.

**Typing feedback is sacred:** responsive per-keystroke tactile sound, readable live transcription/caret on the typewriter, correct/incorrect letter feedback, target highlights, decisive word capture, clear miss/breach, high-impact Combo/Volley progression. D-068 specifically requires live typewriter preview; ensure desk render depth never covers it. The owner disliked a Nov 20 preview that lost the main-build tactile catch/typing feel; **audit main and preview and preserve/restore the satisfying main-build behavior**. Never let cosmetics change word hitboxes, fall physics, input responsiveness, targeting, legibility or typing difficulty. Reduced motion and accessibility matter. Wind gusts may sway parchment/candles *visually only*. UI communicates Ward stability AND scarred maximum clearly, not just numeric text.

## Audio is CORE GAMEPLAY, not later polish
Retain/rebuild **warm, weighty mechanical typewriter keys** (brass, wood, ink, soft magical resonance), not brittle high-frequency clicks, shrill synth beeps or generic key sounds. Word completion must have a satisfying mechanical-magical release. Perfect captures, Combo, Volley, high-risk survival, Rune and big Harvest payoff gain carefully layered, rhythmically coherent effects; don't drown rapid typing in noise. Each spell has intuitive physical timbre: Slow time-warp, Ice crystal stasis, Wind airflow and stressed Ward, Fire combustion, Shock electrical arcs, Barrier resonant seal, Alter transformation, Harvest extraction/cash-out. Ambient workshop sounds can reinforce candles, gears, paper and magical instruments.

**Music:** owner liked the existing RuneScape-like magical atmosphere. Preserve its sense of wonder. Menu music is slower/warmer. Gameplay soundtrack is dynamically layered with field population, arrival pressure, surges, Combo/Volley, danger/recovery, and end-of-level release. Prior Ball X Pit reference means *evolving intensity*, NOT copying its music or visual style. Build toward an exhilarating climax without constant maximal volume or fatigue. Rhythm supports typing, not forced beat matching. Audit and preserve existing music engine where good; improve where needed. Music, SFX, Announcer **independent persisted volume sliders**. Browser audio unlock must work.

**Grand Wizard announcer:** overarching mentor and eventual recorded theatrical voice for major Runes/milestones, not nonstop chatter. **Robotic browser SpeechSynthesis was explicitly rejected (D-052)**. DO NOT re-enable synthetic voice. Silent subtitled placeholder lines are acceptable until owner-approved recorded audio exists. Don't claim voice acting is ready.

## Gameplay-world cohesion
Single player-owned apprentice; no six-character playable roster, wizard passives, sigil class powers or wardrobe system (shelved D-045). Grand Wizard oversees the adventure; eight distinct spell-specialist **Master Wizards** act as NPC mentors. Stages may evolve into their training courses. Teach through forgiving physical demonstrations, purposeful pressure, discovery opportunities, and mastery, with light optional guidance; do not force lecture tutorials or reveal every combination. The old five-stage, 20-level campaign is temporary technical scaffolding, **not final narrative law**. Don't rewrite entire campaign prematurely; deliver one compelling Wind Master slice plus sandbox.

Spell identities: Slow→Volley timing, Ice→Precision Combo, Barrier→Ward/score insurance, Alter→word simplification preserving original earned base points, Fire→base-point reset (Combustion Chain unapproved), Shock→seeded spatial gamble (odds unresolved), Harvest→reliable full-board Combo×Volley cash-out consuming Volley, Wind→defensive recovery and repeatable risky All-In with progressively scarred Ward maximum. D-083 supersedes once-only Wind. Wind+Harvest and Wind+Shock should work through common score rules, not arbitrary special-case bans. See mechanics handoff and D-084 for experimental numbers. No new mana meter, scroll bank, forced grammar system, mandatory water mechanics or new spell economy without separate decision.
