## Game identity: non-negotiable
- Magical typing combat. Falling scrolls are typed/captured; missed words threaten the Ward. Player controls a single apprentice.
- **Physical, intuitive sandbox:** fire melts ice where implemented; Slow slows falling; Wind affects air/environment; players should be encouraged to experiment. Prefer shared systemic rules over spell-pair exceptions.
- **Pressure-driven utility (D-073):** same spell is tempting offensively in easy moments, offers a real tradeoff at medium pressure, and becomes defensive under high pressure. Expert aggressive play should remain possible.
- **Strategic predictability (D-063):** uncertain encounters, understandable rules. Seed randomness, deterministic replay. Do not manipulate ordinary word generation to force achievements, Runes, or scoring setups.
- One primary offensive/scoring identity per spell; don't fabricate perfect word captures, Combo, or Volley merely by casting. Real player execution earns progress.
- The menu Codex may explain exact rules; avoid constant in-run formula tutorials or predicted score displays. UI should celebrate real accomplishments.
- Do not create a separate casting mana meter now. Existing D-057 mana *pool* expands held spell-charge capacity from 8 to 12 with accurate typing. Audit this before inventing more resources. Charges and drop rarity remain constraints.

## Scoring and spell contracts
**Current preview Arcane baseline:** score typed words by word length × 10 × Precision Combo multiplier × Arcane Volley multiplier; inspect exact rounding, current multiplier calculation, eligibility, and special captures in code. Two flawless captures ignite Volley at 1.2×; subsequent successful timely captures progress it. Empty actionable field pauses its opportunity clock with bounded one-time re-engagement grace. Slow makes Volley opportunity clock run at 80% normal rate, nonstacking; spell casts can refresh an active Volley window once between captures, without adding captures; Hardcore clean typed spell earns Combo/points but no quota/Volley. Preserve and test these rules unless a later approved decision explicitly changes them.

**Slow — Volley specialty.** Keep 8-second falling slowdown, stacks on world motion, and nonstacking 80% Volley opportunity clock. No free score multiplier.

**Ice — Precision Combo specialty (D-077).** Freeze motion and spawning 8s, typing remains responsive. **Change current implementation:** Volley opportunity clock should continue while Ice is active (except when field empty under Flow Preservation); Slow remains distinct. Within a freeze, consecutive perfectly typed enemy captures award +2, +4, +8, then +8 Combo per word, instead of ordinary +1. A typo breaks the Focus sequence but already earned Combo remains. Corrected captures do not qualify. Choose deterministic reset behavior for recasts/overlaps; document prototype assumption and test. Rune theme Frostbound Focus.

**Barrier — score insurance, provisional accepted direction from discussion.** Existing cast grants 2 shield layers against Ward breaches. Prototype shared layers also protecting against a *corrected typing mistake* that would otherwise damage Combo/scoring streak, consuming one layer for either threat; don't consume for harmless backspace within existing grace rules. Protected corrected capture is **not perfect** and never earns Ice Focus. Audit actual penalty events before implementing; avoid arbitrary extra multipliers. Rune Unbroken candidate.

**Alter — difficulty shaping.** Preserve its real Hardcore defensive utility. Prototype keeping the *original* word's full base point value when successfully typing a shortened transformed word; no free capture, Combo, or Volley. Audit whether existing 8-second easy-future-spawn effect should remain; default preserve it for first prototype rather than silently delete. Rune Adaptation candidate.

**Fire — base-point reset.** Current full-board burn yields base points, no quota progress and no Combo/Volley bonuses. Owner prefers exploring spatial **Combustion Chain** eventually; mechanics unapproved. **Do not implement new Fire propagation in this vertical slice.**

**Shock — seeded high-variance gamble (D-080).** Guaranteed first strike, possible spatial/density-sensitive seeded chain propagation, claims words for score and quota. Should cash out existing earned Combo and possibly Volley, never create typing streaks. Shock can benefit from Wind All-In using common payout modifier. Exact arc odds/caps not decided: use labeled, conservative preview tuning and deterministic tests; avoid claiming final balance. Rune Chain Reaction candidate.

**Harvest — reliable full-board cash-out.** Capture all current words, award **100% of their combined original base points × earned Combo multiplier × active Volley multiplier**, count all toward quota, do **not** add Combo or Volley captures. **Consume active Volley after applying its multiplier**; Combo persists. Wind All-In modifier should apply too. Unlike Shock's gamble, Harvest is predictable. No Clean Streak multiplier substitution. Rune Reap What You Sow candidate. Beware board clearing, empty-field grace, end-of-level and score-order edge cases.

**Wind — Ward risk / extraordinary scoring opportunity (D-083).** Current implementation merely heals 50 instability points and resets accelerated word speeds; treat as legacy, not canonical. Ward is represented as instability 0=100% stable, 100=collapsed; critical begins at instability 75. Add an explicit maximum recoverable Ward capacity, current stability, scar accumulation, and wager state. A Wind cast when Ward is at its **current maximum** may activate All-In, sacrificing stability and permanently reducing that maximum for the current scope. Future Wind casts can heal to the **new cap** and, if the cap is reached and sufficient capacity remains, activate another wager. Never arbitrarily ban repeats per level/stage; natural cost, available charges, and danger limit them. Normal scars provisionally reset at next level; Hardcore scars provisionally persist through 20-level stage; ensure actual Ward stability isn't magically restored on scar expiry in Hardcore. Normal already resets instability each level; Hardcore carries instability with recovery after levels 5/10/15. **All healing paths must obey cap**, including milestones. Need explicit rule for whether a Wind cast at cap always wagers or whether player can choose defensive-only; document/test intuitive behavior. Repeated Wind during active scoring window must not multiply bonuses exponentially; refresh/replace at most. Wind scoring modifier should apply to **eligible typed scores and spell cash-outs**, including Harvest and Shock, through a shared deterministic scoring rule. Never ban Wind+Harvest merely for being powerful. Consequence persists after instant cash-out. Use tunable constants and bounded seeded tests to prevent runaway scores, not hidden special-case exclusions. Wind gust is visual-only (scroll tilt, candles, particles), preserving hitboxes, actual trajectories, speed, input, and reduced-motion accessibility. Rune/theme Final Wager candidate.

**Important unresolved numeric balance:** exact Ward scar increment, sacrifice amount, minimum safe cap for further wagers, Wind score bonus/window, Hardcore failure rules, Shock chain odds, and interaction between multiple scoring windows. Do **not** present these as approved final values. For first preview use a coherent clearly labeled experimental configuration, document exact numbers and reasoning in a prototype tuning note, expose simple tuning constants, test extremes. Escalate only a decision that blocks coherent implementation. Avoid complicated bespoke economies.

## Campaign / meta-game vision (D-083)
The **Grand Wizard** is overarching mentor, narrator, announcer, and presence. Distinct **Master Wizards**, each expert in a spell, guide apprenticeship training courses. Each course teaches the spell's physical properties, defensive and offensive applications, strategic costs, and possible combinations through staged encounters. The master guides but does not prescribe every solution; **discovery is central**. Apprentice may discover Wind+Harvest before being taught and should feel clever, perhaps recognized by the mentor. Teaching order can differ: Wind might first demonstrate healing then scoring, or reverse; Slow might start with Volley. The current 5-stage × 20-level campaign is **temporary scaffolding**, not a locked future narrative structure. Do not rebuild the full campaign yet. Build a small, skippable, representative Wind Master training slice with Grand Wizard framing, gentle introductory use, pressure tradeoff, optional emergent discovery, and a mastery challenge. Keep narration brief and nonintrusive. Placeholder text/portraits acceptable; don't invent final names, art, or canon lore. Don't lock Wind behind a stage if that prevents testing; provide sandbox/test access.

## Specific implementation traps
- Existing `SpellRegistry.ts` Wind always invokes `restoreWard(50)` and `resetWordSpeeds()`; route through a single authoritative Wind cast decision. Current code and comments disagree whether Hardcore Wind halves instability or heals 50. **Audit, then update code + docs together.**
- `CampaignMode.ts` uses instability carry and milestone healing; save model lacks scar fields. Migrate optional fields safely for older saves.
- Existing Ice **pauses** Volley opportunity clock; D-077 explicitly reverses this.
- Harvest currently describes half-points × Combo/Clean Streak; new direction is full base × Combo × **Volley**, consume Volley. Ensure no double score, rounding discrepancy, quota overrun, or accidental Volley refresh from the Harvest cast.
- The preview Arcane system coexists with legacy scoring, achievements, and duel/replay. Do not break legacy/duel paths or incorrectly describe legacy stats as current scoring.
- Existing `docs/sandbox-interactions.md` is older and contradictory; revise or annotate after code changes.
- Existing D-082 says one wager per scope, **superseded by D-083**. Never silently reintroduce that limit.
- The owner wants exciting power, not artificial prohibitions: balance Wind+Harvest via visible resource costs, cap damage, time, charge supply, and earned multipliers. Preserve combinations.

## Definition of done for first meaningful prototype
A player can (a) use Wind defensively, (b) deliberately enter a dangerous All-In from full current Ward capacity, (c) exploit it by typing and by Wind+Harvest, (d) recover to scarred cap and attempt another wager when physically viable, (e) understand why a later wager is riskier, (f) feel the different Normal/Hardcore carryover, and (g) experience one small Wind-master apprenticeship journey with optional discovery. Core score/clock/save regressions pass; preview deploy is verified. **Do not claim all eight spells are finalized.**
