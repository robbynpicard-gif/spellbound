# PILLAR 1 — GAMEPLAY

## DECIDED — identity, player skill and core loop
- TypeL is an **exhilarating, highly replayable skill-based typing combat game**: falling enchanted word-scrolls threaten the Ward; the apprentice types/captures them; mistakes and misses matter; spell charges allow consequential intervention. A premium modern typing-game successor in *feel*, not a clone of another game's art or assets. Keep typing instantaneous, word targeting readable, and decisions consequential. The goal is "one more run" through mastery, not gambling or coercive loops.
- The core must not be distorted to generate optional Runes/Triumphs/grammar medals (D-058). Ordinary word generation is not biased to fabricate achievement sequences; observe actual capture order. Preserve seeded RNG, fixed-step determinism, replay consistency and transparent rules.
- **Strategic predictability (D-063):** encounter outcomes may vary; mechanical rules, arithmetic, triggers, physical interactions and risk must be consistent and inspectable. A player's valid strategy should work when its stated conditions are met. Main-menu Codex can explain formulas; gameplay should not show speculative future score projections or endless calculation tutorials.
- **Pressure-driven utility (D-073):** spells have coherent identities. At low pressure they invite offensive skill expression, at medium pressure meaningful offense-vs-survival tradeoffs, at high pressure natural defensive value. Skilled players retain the right to gamble aggressively under pressure. Don't force artificial offensive/defensive spell modes by difficulty.
- **Physical intuitive magic (D-069):** Slow slows movement, Ice freezes physical word motion, Fire/ice/wind/world props react coherently where implemented, typing itself remains responsive. Favor shared physical/systemic laws and emergent spell combinations rather than special-case bans. *Potential* future fire melting ice or water conducting Shock are intuitive candidates, NOT automatically implemented or fully specified.
- Spell scrolls grant charges; one shared held-charge pool with accuracy-based capacity (base 8; 9 at 25 clean captures, 10 at 50, 11 at 100, 12 at 200). Wrong Enter/missed word collapses capacity to 8 and excess charges are lost; corrected typo does not shrink it (D-057). **No separate mana meter** is approved. Spell rarity/charge supply are strategic resources. Normal can cast via configured 1–8 keys or typed spell name+Enter; Hardcore requires typing spell names (D-049). Spellbook maps keys; ordinary falling words must not collide with spell command prefixes.
- **Arcane Precision Combo and Volley are distinct.** Current experimental Arcane typed-score baseline: `round(letters × 10 × Precision Combo × Arcane Volley)` before additional eligible shared modifiers; confirm actual code/rounding. A successful word capture, not merely casting, is the core skill event. No spell should silently fabricate perfect captures, Combo or Volley. Hardcore correctly typed clean spell names can earn Combo and length-based points but do not count as captured enemies, quota or Volley (D-072). Corrected or direct casts don't receive that reward.
- **Arcane Volley two-capture ignition (D-060):** first perfect capture arms; second timely perfect capture activates ×1.2. Never award multiplier on every isolated submission. Word-length-aware Normal timing prototype `52+13×letters` ticks; Hardcore `34+10×letters` (D-068). Empty actionable screen preserves the opportunity clock and allows **one bounded 0.4 s re-engagement grace** per successful capture (D-067), without changing spawn pacing. A successful charged spell during active Volley can refresh the window **once between word captures**, not raise multiplier or add captures (D-072; supersedes D-071 1.5 s pause). Slow runs Volley clock at 80% speed, noncompounding, giving 25% longer real-time opportunity (D-069). **Ice must NOT freeze Volley clock** under D-077; older D-067/070 descriptions of Ice clock pause are superseded.
- **Volley long arc (D-059) is accepted as a design goal, NOT current final implementation:** visible early progression candidates ×1.2/1.4/1.6/1.8, earned eruption around ×2, sustained Surge toward ~×5, then increasingly difficult soft-cap increments +0.1 without arbitrary hard ceiling. Exact milestones, durations, ignition tuning, decay and ceilings await playtesting; do not silently replace current two-word prototype with untested ladder.
- **Presentation of performance (D-062/064/065):** accurate full rules in main-menu Codex; during play preserve wonder and readable achievements; between levels a short celebratory snapshot (cumulative stage score, level score, accuracy, WPM, peak Volley), not a spreadsheet/formula lecture. Detailed run/stage review at end, grounded in actually tracked stats. Words continue on field between levels within a run, subject to save/load semantics. Runes reward mastery rather than just casting (D-076); final names and thresholds are open.

## DECIDED — eight spell identities (not all numeric details)
1. **Slow / Temporal Distortion:** 8 s world slowdown (existing speed factor ~0.6) and nonstacking 80% Volley opportunity clock. Player typing unaffected. Offensive identity = sustained Volley; defensive identity = more time to type. Do not add a free score multiplier.
2. **Ice / Frostbinding (D-077):** freezes falling word motion and spawning while typing continues; current accepted later duration 8 s, but old Nov 19 six-second stackable prototype exists historically. During freeze, each consecutive *perfect* word capture increases Precision Combo by **+2, +4, +8, +8…**, instead of +1; typo breaks *Focus sequence*; earned Combo should not be retroactively deleted merely because Ice expires. Ice does **not** stop Volley opportunity time (except normal empty-field preservation). Recast/overlap details are testable.
3. **Barrier / Arcane Shield:** existing 2 shield layers per cast intercept Ward breaches; its proposed score-preservation identity is **insurance** against mistakes. D-084 experimentally implements one layer absorbing corrected-typo penalties in an entry while keeping capture imperfect; this is a promising **prototype choice**, not final locked behavior. Never make protected mistakes qualify as perfect Ice Focus.
4. **Alter / Transmutation:** simplifies difficult words and retains defensive value, especially Hardcore. Preserve **original base score** on successful typing of a transformed word; transformation does not itself count as a capture. D-084 preserves an 8-second easy-word spawning window. Exact simplification length (older 2–3 or 3–5) and overlap require code audit/tuning.
5. **Fire / Incineration:** defensive emergency burn for **base points**, no Combo/Volley credit and no quota credit. A D-057 rule limited burning to words below the top fifth of the screen, conflicting with older 'whole field' text; audit current behavior and do not silently erase that decision. Fire redesign is NOT approved.
6. **Shock / Chain Lightning (D-080):** guaranteed initial strike; high-variance **seeded, spatial/density-sensitive chain propagation** can cash out earned scoring position. Subsequent arcs are uncertain but rules and replay deterministic. Counts claimed words toward quota; never fabricates Combo/Volley. Exact odds, arcs, cap, Combo/Volley inclusion not final. D-084 **temporarily retains fixed three arcs**, which does NOT fulfill the future high-variance direction.
7. **Harvest:** deterministic full-board capture, **100% original base points × earned Combo × active Volley**, plus any active eligible Wind modifier, counted toward quota. Consumes active Volley *after* applying its value, does not advance Combo or Volley, and does not fabricate perfect captures. D-084 implemented prototype. Older half-base × Clean Streak Harvest is superseded.
8. **Wind / Arcane Purge / Final Wager (D-083):** reusable defensive Ward restoration and removal of mistake-accelerated word speed; when Ward is at its **current recoverable maximum**, a cast can trigger an offensive All-In, dramatically reducing stability and **progressively scarring the maximum**. Another Wind charge can restore Ward up to its new cap and permit another wager if physically viable. No arbitrary once-per-level or once-per-stage ban. Consequence persists after instant Wind+Harvest payoff. Wind scoring opportunity should apply through **shared eligible scoring** to typed captures and Harvest/Shock, not typed-only exceptions. Multiple active Wind buffs cannot multiply exponentially; replace/refresh at most. Wind gust cosmetic: sway scroll/candles/particles, no changed hitboxes, trajectories or readability. Normal scars provisionally reset per level; Hardcore scars provisionally last stage, and expiry restores capacity but not lost stability. See D-084 for **EXPERIMENTAL** current numbers: heal-to-cap below max, at-cap automatic wager, Ward to 25%, cap 100→85→65→40, ×2 for 10 s, no more wager below 40%, Normal breach ends bonus, Hardcore typo/wrong Enter/breach ends bonus. These values, trigger ambiguity, difficulty rules and inability to opt out at cap are **not owner-locked**.

## EXPLORATORY — gameplay
- Volley progression to long high-skill Surge/soft cap; final timing and how much reward can be earned in sustained play.
- Fire **Combustion Chain** spatial propagation is preferred *future investigation* (D-079); Controlled Burn, Lingering Embers and danger-weighted base points are other historical candidates. No final propagation or burn payout rule.
- Shock seeded chain probabilities, proximity rules, maximum and cash-out relation to Volley; tests must prevent runaway multipliers without banning logical synergies.
- Wind wager size, cap scar sequence, bonus duration and scale, defensive-only cast choice at cap, interaction with multiple charges and active window, Hardcore mistake severity. Repeated wagers are direction; exact 3-wager D-084 limit emerges from tuning, not an approved hard cap.
- Barrier typo insurance exact scope, typo semantics, other defensive uses; Alter easy-word window length; Ice Focus interaction with recasts and corrected entries.
- Grammar/parts-of-speech Triumphs, word-category achievements, rare Runes, player scroll bank, future elemental interactions (fire melting ice, water dousing fire, conduction) and additional spell combinations. None authorize altering ordinary word generation or adding a new economy.
- Encounter patterns: Swarm and Dangerous Word, randomized but recognizable stage identity, surges, boss challenges, skill-based varied word lengths, special encounters. They were requested/approved for exploration or implementation, but final frequency, encounter taxonomy and content are not canonized. Existing stage targets and pacing are tuning baselines, not immutable.
- Potential multiplayer/duel is historical aspiration; current priority is solo skill loop. No new multiplayer infrastructure without decision.

# PILLAR 2 — VISUAL COMPOSITION

## DECIDED — creative intent and functional requirements
- **Arcane/Alchemical Renaissance** world: warm parchment, ink, illuminated manuscripts, dark carved wood, aged brass/copper, glass alchemical vessels, candles, wax seals, gears, runes, eccentric scholarly wizardry and lived-in magical laboratories/libraries/workshops. Magic feels physical and material. This is the approved **world language**, not a command to use a particular rendering technique.
- **Critical owner correction:** **TypeL is NOT defined as pixel art.** Pixel art was an early possibility. No approved final choice between pixel art, painted illustration, vector, 2D, 2.5D, 3D, camera perspective, lighting engine, shader pipeline, or asset production method. Do not give any technique priority because old assets happen to use it.
- The player's wizard is seated/working at a magical writing station, transcribing falling enchanted scrolls to protect a Ward. A **prominent tactile mechanical/alchemical typewriter or desk** is central to the fantasy, with readable **central Ward/falling-scroll battlefield** and spell rack/controls integrated into the world. Earlier owner discussion described central field ~75%, wizard lower left, typewriter bottom-center, spell rack right, alchemical instruments around. These are **composition references, not locked coordinates or final layout**.
- Scrolls/words must remain readable and targetable at high typing speed. Strong text contrast, stable input, distinguishable spell scrolls, clear targeting, feedback for correct/incorrect prefixes, captures, breaches and impending danger. **D-068 live transcription** with caret on the physical typewriter must never be covered by scene depth or props.
- HUD communicates current Ward stability, **scarred recoverable cap**, Combo, Volley, held spell charges, score and progress at a glance. Prefer diegetic mechanical/rune indicators where readable, but never hide vital state merely to appear immersive.
- Dynamic physical feedback: keys depress, typebars/clunks, ink, parchment, seal, candles, ambient machinery, elemental spell effects, earned Combo/Volley/All-In intensity. Spells have distinct identities and responsive effects; cosmetic Wind gust cannot move actual gameplay hitboxes/scroll trajectories. Reduced-motion support, minimal disruptive camera shake, and no full-screen effects that stop typing.
- **Single apprentice**, Grand Wizard mentor/announcer and individual spell-specialist NPC Master Wizards belong to same believable world, not unrelated tutorial popups. Final character appearances remain open.
- Maintain the satisfying original/main-build combat and legibility while overhauling presentation. Current preview aesthetics are not automatically the destination.

# PILLAR 3 — AUDIO

## DECIDED — sound and music principles
- **Audio is a pillar of typing gameplay**, not optional polish. Preserve/restore the satisfying **main-build typing and word-capture feedback** when newer previews lose it. Compare main and preview in actual play; don't claim audio quality based on a build passing.
- Keys should feel **weighty, warm, brassy, wooden and mechanical**: wooden thunk + brass typebar ring + subtle magical resonance, immediate and repeatable at high WPM. Rounder voices, gentle low-pass/low-end weight rather than shrill square-wave clicks (D-045). Sound timing reinforces typing precision.
- Word captures need a **distinct satisfying mechanical-magical completion**, with graduated tasteful layers for exceptional execution, Combo, Volley, spell casts and Rune triumphs. A mistype/miss communicates what happened clearly without painful or excessive sound.
- Spell sound identities should map to physical properties: Slow time distortion; Ice crystalline lock; Fire combustion; Wind air pressure/gust and stressed Ward; Shock lightning; Barrier resonant protective seal; Alter ink/material change; Harvest sweeping extraction/cash-out. Big Wind+Harvest payoff can be spectacular, not a constant deafening effect.
- Music: owner **liked the RuneScape-like magical wonder** of earlier score (D-051); retain that spirit. Distinct **slower warmer menu theme** (D-054), dynamic in-game music driven by pressure and progression: scroll density, surges, level intensity, Combo/Volley, Ward danger, recovery, bosses, and relief. Older implementation added ticking/ostinato/drums and up to ~28% drive; these are prior tuning, not final composition. Ball X Pit reference concerns **evolving energy**, not copied music or aesthetics.
- Separate persistent **Music, SFX and Announcer** channels with individual 0–100 volume and on/off (D-053), browser audio-unlock compliance, quick mute options. Test latency, overlap, clipping, accessibility and long-session fatigue.
- **Robotic browser TTS announcer is rejected** (D-052). The Grand Wizard can eventually provide a theatrical recorded voice with character, sparse major milestones/Rune calls; until real approved voice lines exist, use silent text/subtitle placeholders. No invented claim that voice lines are recorded. Announcer must not speak over every typed word.

## EXPLORATORY — audio
- Final instrument palette, composition, tempo, exact key sound samples vs synthesis, recording and processing chain, spatial audio, spell-layer mixes, pressure-state thresholds, whether music reacts more to Volley or Ward and how much it crescendos.
- Owner recording voice and offline pitch/EQ/compression/saturation/reverb treatment for Grand Wizard; NPC Master Wizard voices, dialogue cadence and personalities, and how voice interacts with teaching. Not approved as final voice assets.
- Specific feedback motifs for Focus, All-In scar, charge overflow, Runes, Shock gamble, end-of-stage performance, and progressive music transitions. Build a cue map and playtest, don't invent final sound bible.
- Prior synthesizer/Web Audio architecture is usable technical foundation, not a creative restriction against recorded sound or alternative production methods.

## SUPERSEDED / NOT PERMISSIBLE — audio
- Browser SpeechSynthesis pitched-down announcer as shipping feature (D-051 → rejected D-052).
- Bright cheap beeps, brittle key clicks, nonresponsive flat music, constant max-intensity stingers, or a silent typing experience presented as finished.
- Treating the original D-minor score, F-major menu loop, exact 28% tempo increase, synth-only architecture or old hit-stop as immutable final sound design.
- Tying announcer volume to SFX only, removing independent audio sliders, or adding constant speech without real recorded lines.
- Assuming a mechanically correct preview is ready for holistic game-feel testing if tactile audio and reactive soundtrack are absent.

# PILLAR 4 — PROGRESSION / META-GAME

## DECIDED — journey, modes and player agency
- **One player-owned Apprentice** who grows into mastery; **Grand Wizard** is overarching mentor, personality and eventual announcer; **eight Master Wizards** each specialize in one of the eight spells and guide apprenticeship courses. This is the current narrative/progression vision (D-083), NOT an eight-character playable roster.
- Teach spells through **situations and discovery**, not lectures: introduction of physical property, useful applications, increasingly demanding offensive/defensive tradeoffs, emergent combinations and mastery challenges. Teaching order varies by spell. Wind might teach recovery before All-In or vice versa; encourage player to discover Wind+Harvest early and acknowledge clever experimentation. Masters are knowledgeable, not omniscient; no one prescribed solution. Skippable/replayable guidance and sandbox access are desirable.
- **Normal and Hardcore** remain meaningful gameplay modes. Normal is accessible and more forgiving; Hardcore rewards clean typed execution and carries greater Ward risk. Exact final curriculum differences and progression rules remain to be designed.
- Current campaign's **five stages per mode × twenty levels, final boss at level 20** (D-047), per-stage unlocks, carryover, save/continue, stage best scores, post-boss Endless and Practice, is **implemented/provisional scaffolding**, not the definitive required stage length or narrative order. Don't rip it out before replacement exists; don't canonize it. Existing Hardcore Ward carry and milestone recovery must honor Wind scars. Normal Ward resets between levels. Boss wind-down secures victory after timer, stops spawns and clears field; words carry between levels in same active run (D-055/057). Post-boss Endless ramps and records best score per stage (D-057). These remain testable gameplay baselines, not permanent content structure.
- The player must be able to understand and test progress: accurate Codex, minimal between-level summary, richer end-stage review, clear unlocked spells and resource state, practice/sandbox and accessible stage navigation. Runes/Triumphs recognize demonstrated mastery, not spell button presses or manipulated spawns.
- **Experimental existing vertical slice (D-084):** four skippable Wind Master lessons framed by Grand Wizard, plus a 40-charge sandbox; no progression, stats, Runes or Triumph recording. This is an implemented **placeholder training prototype**, not final course length, character dialogue or curriculum.
- Story should be implied through world and experience rather than long exposition; narrative supports addictive gameplay, not interrupts it.

## EXPLORATORY — progression
- Whether each specialist master's course is one stage, several shorter lessons, or a different structure; curriculum order, mastery assessments, graduation, optional discovery achievements, master personalities, dialogue, visual scenes, player progression, boss concepts, pacing and difficulty curves.
- Apprenticeship-based spell unlocks and advanced interactions, meaningful replay challenges, optional Runes/Triumphs/grammar feats and a bank of captured scrolls; do not implement new resource economies until approved.
- Whether a wizard reacts to player-discovered interactions, how hints fade with mastery, narrative framing of Ward scarring, and long-term Grand Wizard examinations or combined-spell trials.
- Swarm/Dangerous Word encounters, themed stage variance and stage-specialist environmental rules; no final generation or boss taxonomy.
- Existing Normal/Hardcore stage WPM targets (historical Normal start ~23 then 29/35/42/48/56; Hardcore start ~35 then 40/48/56/64/72) are *implemented balance baselines*, not permanent promise. Preserve until a tested alternative is accepted.
- Additional multiplayer/duel and persistent character development are longer-term possibilities; not a current green light for feature expansion.

## SUPERSEDED / NOT PERMISSIBLE — progression
- Old four-chapter journey and boss spell pick-of-three as final structure (D-016/025/044), and rigid five-stage × twenty-level content treated as forever decided. The latter is still useful code, not canon.
- Six selectable wizards, sigil powers, wizard classes, familiars, cosmetics/wardrobe XP roster as active progression (D-042/043 → shelved D-045/048).
- Forcing every learner through long mandatory tutorials, making every spell interaction explicitly explained, preventing advanced experimentation before its assigned lesson, or restricting a sandbox solely to maintain an arbitrary curriculum.
- Automatically changing word generation, stage pacing or core skill to increase Rune/grammar reward frequency (D-058).
- Treating placeholder Grand Wizard/Wind Master text and four lessons as final lore, final characters or finished campaign.
